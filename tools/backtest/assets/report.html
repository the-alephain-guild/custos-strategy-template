Time and marker rules · UTC candle close

Candle timestamps are their close in UTC. Signals use observation time; fills use execution time. Markers align forward to a candle close, never backward to a pivot. Original times and prices remain in the event table. Signals appear above candles; fills appear below them. A count marker groups multiple events for display only.

Signals & execution

Each Signal is a parent. Expand its fill count to inspect every partial execution or order. FILL1.2 means the second fill of SIG1; each child keeps its actual execution time. A group remains visible if its signal or any fill is in view.

EL = enter long · XL = exit long · ES = enter short · XS = exit short. A signal may have no fill. One fill may close a position and open its opposite.
