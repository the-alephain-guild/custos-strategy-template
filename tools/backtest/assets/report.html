Signals & execution

Use the arrow beside a signal number to expand its fills. Table numbers omit prefixes: chart FILL1.2 is fill 2 under signal 1. Position before and after are signed net quantities. Unknown order types were not recorded; they are never inferred. A group remains visible if its signal or any fill is in view.

EL = enter long · XL = exit long · ES = enter short · XS = exit short. A signal may have no fill. One fill may close a position and open its opposite.

Net P&L and return rules

Realized P&L is this fill's new closing profit or loss minus its entire fee. Opening fees are deducted immediately, once. Cumulative P&L covers this instrument's full chronological fill history, independent of filters and signal groups.

Return uses the closed quantity's weighted-average entry notional and deducts its allocated entry and exit fees. Entry fees already affected earlier fill P&L and are not deducted twice from cumulative P&L. Pure opening fills have no closing return. Values exclude leverage, unrealized P&L, funding and transfers. Missing economics or nonzero fees in another currency show Unavailable; no exchange rate is assumed.
